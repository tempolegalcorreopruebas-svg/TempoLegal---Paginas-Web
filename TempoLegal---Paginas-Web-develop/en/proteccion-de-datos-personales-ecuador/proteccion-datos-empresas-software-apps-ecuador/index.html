An external DPO who works with your product, technology and business teams, taking into account your responsibilities towards users and clients.

In Ecuador the role is called DPD (Delegado de Protección de Datos). DPO stands for Data Protection Officer, its English name.

The DPO informs, advises and oversees. Your organization decides and executes. The proposal defines products, follow-up, audits and duties to prevent conflicts of interest.

Explore the advantages of an external DPO
Does a software or IT company need a DPO?

The rules include private legal entities that regularly offer or provide IT services and act as controllers or processors. We review your activity, legal form and actual processing to determine the applicable case. The obligation is not ruled out just because you have few users or employees.

DPO Regulation · art. 10.13 (Spanish) ↗

QUESTIONS FROM MANAGEMENT, PRODUCT AND IT

Answers for making decisions.

Are we controllers or processors of our clients’ data?

It is determined for each processing operation and by what you actually do. You may be the controller of your staff’s data and your business contacts, and a processor when you process information on your clients’ instructions. If you use that data for your own purposes, what changes must be reviewed; a contractual label does not replace that analysis.

Does using a foreign cloud always mean an international transfer?

It is not determined by the server’s location alone. The parties, their roles, purposes and instructions, and the processing conditions must be analysed. A processing mandate and a transfer are different concepts: we review the specific flow and the applicable safeguards. SPDP non-binding technical opinion (Spanish) ↗

Is a confidentiality agreement with the client enough?

Confidentiality is one piece. When there is a processing mandate, the instructions, subject matter and conditions of processing, measures, sub-processors, assistance with rights requests and incidents, and return or deletion at the end must also be regulated. We review what has already been signed before proposing changes.

What if we host encrypted information and don’t read it?

It should not be automatically concluded that there are no obligations. We review storage, operation, access, control and instructions, as well as the obligations assumed towards the client. The assessment depends on the real processing.

Can our CTO or head of systems be the DPO?

The position or technical knowledge is not enough. Requirements, duties and independence must be verified. Administering or deciding on systems that are later overseen can create conflicts of interest. The technical lead can act as a liaison with the DPO.

Do we have to change platforms or redo all our documents?

Not by default. We review what already exists and distinguish what to keep, correct or complete. If a practice or configuration requires changes, we explain it with a priority and an owner. A prior audit may be necessary and must be included in the scope.

Does it include code review or security testing?

Not automatically. A non-intrusive technical review can analyse agreed evidence and controls. Penetration testing, code review, forensics, development, configuration or external certification require a specific definition; the legal review does not by itself demonstrate the product’s security.

What do you review if we add AI, an API or migrate data?

The new purposes, necessary data, roles, flows, providers, instructions and risks. It may be necessary to update notices, contracts and measures, or carry out an impact assessment. The work is scoped before the change goes live.

Do you guarantee we will pass client audits or questionnaires?

No. We prepare answers that match your operations and the evidence available, identify gaps and define actions. We do not mark a control as met without support or guarantee the decision of a buyer or auditor.

What must our team provide and how long does it take?

A management liaison and a technical liaison, information on products and providers and existing documentation. The schedule depends on entities, flows, risks and technical work; it is confirmed in the proposal. We do not ask for credentials or real client databases in the initial consultation.

Do the 30 days of support include an ongoing DPO?

They are questions and adjustments within the implementation scope for 30 days from document delivery, at no extra cost. The DPO is a continuity service with its own duties, products and conditions. CumpliDatos is included at no extra cost while you keep that service.

Does the large-scale calculator decide whether we need a DPO?

No. It provides guidance on one of the criteria. The direct appointment cases must also be reviewed, including the regular IT activity of private legal entities acting as controllers or processors. A numerical result does not rule out a sector obligation.
