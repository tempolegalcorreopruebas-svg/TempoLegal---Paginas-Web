OVERSIGHT AND CONTINUITY

Implementing and overseeing
are different roles.

An external Data Protection Officer (DPD or DPO) to advise on and oversee data processing in your healthcare organization, in coordination with management and your clinical and technology leads.

In Ecuador the role is called DPD (Delegado de Protección de Datos). DPO stands for Data Protection Officer, its English name.

We review the obligation to appoint a Data Protection Officer according to your activity and the applicable rules. The DPO advises and oversees independently; the organization keeps its responsibilities.

QUESTIONS ABOUT YOUR OPERATIONS

Answers to help you

Data protection in clinics, medical practices, laboratories and healthcare organizations.

Self-assessment ↗DPO guidance ↗

The tools provide guidance; determining obligations requires reviewing your activity and the applicable sector rules.

Are medical confidentiality and a privacy policy enough?+

They are part of the protection, but access, custody, delivery of information and staff responsibilities must also be organized. We review how data is handled from admission to records, including administrative processes and providers.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Does consent to medical care authorize any use of the data?+

No. Informed consent for a medical intervention and the legal basis for processing personal data serve different purposes. The LOPDP provides for healthcare cases in which consent is not required to process data. That does not automatically authorize advertising, promotional photographs or other uses: each purpose must be assessed and explained.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Who can consult a medical record or receive a copy?+

Access must correspond to authorized roles and preserve confidentiality. To hand over a copy, we verify who is requesting it, their identity and the appropriate representation or authorization. Being a relative of the patient should not be treated as automatic authorization to access all their information.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Can a patient demand that we immediately delete their medical record?+

The request must be handled, but deletion is not automatic when there are retention duties or another applicable legal reason. We review what information must be kept, for how long and how to restrict its uses. We do not set a single retention period for all files without reviewing the rules and the type of document.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗LOPDP and exercise of rights (Spanish) ↗
Does having medical software solve compliance?+

Not on its own. The provider’s role, access, security, instructions and contractual terms must also be reviewed. If it processes data on behalf of the institution, that relationship requires an appropriate contract; if other countries or providers are involved, this must be analysed expressly.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Can we send results by WhatsApp or email?+

The channel requires a specific assessment: the correct recipient, the necessary information, access and confidentiality measures. Before enabling it, we define the procedure with your team. For an initial commercial enquiry, do not send us medical records, results or documents that identify patients.

Does our clinic or practice need a DPO?+

It is not decided by size alone. The rules cover health-system actors required to keep medical records and set out an exception for professionals in private practice. We review how you operate and the other applicable cases before determining the obligation and the appropriate arrangement. The large-scale cases provided for in the rules are also reviewed, including those that qualify directly; it is not resolved just by counting patients. Official opinion on large scale (Spanish) ↗

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
What do we do if information is lost or sent to the wrong patient?+

Activate the incident procedure and coordinate immediately with the internal owners, IT and the DPO, if there is one. Document what happened, preserve evidence and assess the containment measures and any notifications required. A breach can affect confidentiality, integrity or availability; it is not limited to a cyberattack.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Do we have to redo everything if we already have policies or a DPO?+

Not necessarily. We start from the existing work and agree on what to keep, update or complete. Implementation, DPO oversight and technical work have separate scopes; the proposal identifies owners, deliverables and additional work before it is carried out.

How do we start and what does our organization receive?+

We start by getting to know your activity, your processes and the work already done. The proposal defines the documents, procedures, training and reviews you need, along with the timeline and fees. The preliminary contact is not an audit and does not require sending patient data.
