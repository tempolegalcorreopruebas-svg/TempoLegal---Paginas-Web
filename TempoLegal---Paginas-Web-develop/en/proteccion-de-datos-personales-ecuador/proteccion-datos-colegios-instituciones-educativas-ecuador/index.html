IMPLEMENTING AND OVERSEEING ARE DIFFERENT TASKS

What your school’s project should cover.

01 / IMPLEMENTATION

System and impact assessment

The systematic processing of children’s and adolescents’ data in educational settings is classified directly as large scale. When special categories are processed on a large scale, an impact assessment is required; it is not ruled out by having few students.

The project must identify risks, instruments, measures, owners and evidence. If you already have an assessment, we review whether it is current and its scope before proposing to redo it.

02 / DPO AND CONTINUITY

An independent DPO

An external Data Protection Officer (DPD or DPO) to support the principal’s office, administration and the areas that use data on students, families and staff.

In Ecuador the role is called DPD (Delegado de Protección de Datos). DPO stands for Data Protection Officer, its English name.

Pre-school, primary and secondary education are expressly included among the activities required to appoint a DPO. The obligation to appoint one does not require the external arrangement.

The school decides and applies; the team produces what was contracted and the DPO advises and oversees. The proposal specifies the appointment, registration, follow-up products and separation of duties.

Explore the advantages of an external DPO
What follow-up does large-scale processing require?

The rules provide for updating the RoPA (RAT) at least annually, an audit at least every twelve months and an annual compliance report. The proposal identifies which of these tasks TempoLegal provides, who carries them out and under what conditions, without confusing execution with oversight.

These rules refer to processing that falls under the regulatory cases. A centre exclusively for adults requires an analysis of its activity and processing.

QUESTIONS FROM YOUR INSTITUTION

Answers to help you
decide clearly.

Questions about students, families, staff and institutional information.

What should a school review to comply with personal data protection?+

It should review how it collects, uses, shares and retains information on students, families and employees. The work covers admissions, enrolment, files, DECE, communications, platforms and security. The principal’s office must coordinate the areas involved: it is not a task for IT alone. We start from your current processes and documents to define priorities and owners.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Is the authorization families sign at enrolment enough?+

A general authorization for any use is not enough. Educational purposes must be distinguished from other uses, such as advertising. When consent is required, it must be specific and informed. The LOPDP allows adolescents aged 15 and over to give explicit consent for their data if they clearly understand the purposes. For younger minors, their representative’s involvement is arranged, always keeping their best interests in mind.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Can we publish photos and videos of students on social media?+

Before publishing, review the purpose, the channels and the appropriate authorization according to age. Enrolment should not become an indiscriminate authorization for advertising. Separate these uses from those needed to provide the educational service and explain how to withdraw consent. You must also assess whether the publication exposes or harms the student, even if there is authorization.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Do schools need a Data Protection Officer?+

Yes: the regulation expressly includes pre-school, primary and secondary education, across their different types of funding and formats; it also covers higher education institutions. The obligation should not be ruled out because the school is small. The DPO may be external and must act independently. Their oversight and advice are different from carrying out the implementation. The proposal must identify both services and who is responsible for them.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Who can access DECE files and health data?+

Only those who need that information for a legitimate role and are authorized. It is not advisable to give teachers or administration general access for convenience. There must be access controls, confidentiality and appropriate channels to share necessary information. Confidentiality does not remove protection duties or the procedures for acting when rights are violated: each communication must respond to its purpose and protocol.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Is the educational platform responsible for everything?+

No. Hiring a platform does not remove the institution’s responsibilities. You need to determine the provider’s role, review instructions and the contract, security, access, location and flow of the data, and what happens when the service ends. If it uses the information for its own purposes, that situation requires an additional review.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Can we use fingerprints or facial recognition for attendance and access?+

Convenience or a signed authorization is not enough. The biometrics rule prohibits, as a general rule, the biometric identification of minors, with an exception when there are no less invasive or risky means and the risk analysis and impact assessment are carried out. The rules on legal basis and consent applicable to the age must also be met. First review non-biometric alternatives and the situation of any installed system.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Do the school’s cameras follow the same rules as facial recognition?+

They are not equivalent processing operations. For ordinary video surveillance, the purpose, proportionate placement, visible information, restricted access and justified retention must be defined. Adding facial identification also requires meeting the specific biometrics conditions. A sign does not authorize any recording or later use.

See the official source (Spanish) ↗
Must we delete all data when a student leaves?+

Not automatically. A distinction must be made between the documentation that must be kept under applicable obligations and the information that is no longer needed. Define criteria by type of file, access and secure deletion; do not apply a single retention period to the entire school archive.

What do we need to start and what will the institution receive?+

We start with a conversation about your campuses, students, processes and systems. We request existing documents and a contact person to coordinate with the principal’s office, administration, DECE and IT. The proposal will specify the assessment, documents and procedures, training, measures to apply and contracted support; as well as owners, deliverables, schedule and fees. If you need an external DPO or additional technical work, it will be expressly identified.

Can we keep using Idukay and WhatsApp with families?

The project does not start from changing your tools by default. We review uses, contracts, access and channels. We define what can be communicated in a group and what should go through an individual or institutional channel, avoiding exposing students’ grades, debts, health or personal situations. If an inappropriate practice or configuration is detected, the corresponding measure is recommended.

Does a small school need an impact assessment?

The systematic processing of children’s and adolescents’ data in educational settings is a direct large-scale case. Large-scale processing of special categories requires an impact assessment. It is not ruled out by the number of students alone; the processing operations, risks and scope of the assessment are identified.

Official SPDP rule on large-scale processing · PDF (Spanish) ↗
What do we do if a parent, representative or authority requests information?

First, identity, standing, purpose and scope of the request are verified. The answer should not be to hand over the entire file by default. We define guidelines for the school office and DECE, review applicable restrictions or documents, delivery channels and logging of the action, without preventing communications that are legally appropriate.

Can the person who administers the school’s systems be the DPO?

An appointment should not be accepted as valid just because the person knows IT. Requirements and conflicts of interest must be verified: administering the systems or deciding on the processing that is later overseen can compromise independence. We review the real role, the appointment and its registration; the technical lead can act as a liaison with the DPO.
