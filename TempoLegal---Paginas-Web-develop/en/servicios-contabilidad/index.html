What needs organizing today?

Starting a company: review activity, obligations, systems and documents to establish an organized accounting base.

Existing operation: we can keep the accounts or review and coordinate with your accountant, defining records, reports, payroll and administrative tasks.

Backlogs or a change of accountant: identify periods, returns, records and available evidence. Regularization and transition have a separate scope from current monthly work.

Accounting as a foundation. Support to suit your operation.

Accounting and tax: records, receipts and withholding reviews, reconciliations, returns and applicable annexes, financial statements, closings and contracted company or municipal obligations. Tax planning reflects the actual activity.

Payroll: agreed payslips, statutory benefits, IESS and Ministry of Labour procedures. The price depends on staff and scope; the employer provides timely updates and retains employment decisions. Specialized legal work is defined separately.

Supervision: review information and outstanding matters, track observations and coordinate accounting, administration and legal advice. Supervising does not automatically mean preparing, recording or filing.

Administrative support: digital archives, task matrices, documented approvals, forwarding invoices and receipts, and agreed intercompany invoicing coordination. Tasks and authorizations are specified.

Recording a payment is not executing it. Accounting and back office do not automatically include treasury, disposal of funds or bank signing powers. Your company retains its decisions and authorizations.

A clear transition. Work that continues.

Understand activity, transaction volume, staff, systems, accounting status and team needs. Define tasks, people, fees, periods and delivery conditions.

Organize documents and access through the indicated channel, identify missing information, process contracted work and communicate reports, observations and next steps.

Your team provides receipts, movements, updates and decisions within the agreed timetable. We manage contracted records, reviews and obligations; you receive the agreed reports, evidence and follow-up at a defined frequency.

Before you engage us

The scope is defined for your company and the decision you need to make.

What does monthly accounting include?

The proposal specifies records, reconciliations, applicable returns and annexes, reports, information delivery and responsibilities. Payroll, prior periods, supervision and administrative support may have their own scope; it is not unlimited administration.

How is the proposal defined?

It depends on the company’s structure, activity, transaction volume, staff, systems, head-office coordination and delegated tasks. Fees, deliverables, reports, periods, closings and each party’s responsibilities are specified before engagement.

Can you work with our existing accountant?

Yes. Keeping accounts and reviewing or supervising an internal accountant are different engagements. We specify who records, prepares, files and reviews, what information each team supplies and how observations are communicated.

Can you regularize backlogs or help change accountants?

Yes. We review periods, returns, records, documents and access to be transferred, then propose a transition or regularization with separate scope and fees. Prior years or months are not automatically part of the current monthly fee.

What does administrative support mean?

Agreed document and coordination tasks may include digital archives, task matrices, documented approvals, forwarding invoices and receipts and intercompany invoicing coordination. Responsibilities, frequency and authorizations are defined; it does not include every business decision or daily management.

Do you execute payments or manage bank accounts?

Recording payments and organizing evidence are different from making transfers. Treasury, disposal of funds and bank signing are not included by default. The company retains decisions and authorizations; powers or representation are reviewed independently.

Are payroll, IESS and employment obligations included?

They can be expressly included based on staff numbers and employer information. Payslips, benefits and applicable procedures are defined in the proposal. Hiring, terminations, disputes and specialist employment advice are scoped separately.

What information must we provide and what reports will we receive?

Relevant receipts, withholding documents, movements and supporting records are required. Channels, delivery dates, periods and reports such as statements, reconciliations and obligation tracking are agreed. Missing information and its effects are identified. Do not send bank statements, payroll, credentials or accounting files in the public enquiry.

Do you use your own system or include invoicing software?

We may use our accounting system or coordinate with the operation’s agreed system. Accounting is distinct from an invoicing licence. SRI authorization, tools, licences, access, backups and the handover at the end of the service are defined.

Does a company without sales still have obligations?

Having no invoices does not itself remove obligations. We review the RUC, tax regime, registered obligations, operation and relevant company and municipal duties. Returns and deadlines are not identical for every company.

Are taxes, fees and authority proceedings included in professional fees?

Company taxes and official fees are separate. Fines, interest, refunds, authority enquiries and control proceedings are not presumed included in an ordinary monthly service. Additional work is proposed in advance. Tax planning does not guarantee savings or freedom from penalties.

Can you keep the accounts of a foreign-owned company?

Yes, with defined Ecuador accounting, tax work and head-office coordination. Foreign participation entails document and obligation checks, not unlimited international advice or an audit. Shareholders do not need Ecuadorian nationality or a visa; residence, work and representation are separate matters.
