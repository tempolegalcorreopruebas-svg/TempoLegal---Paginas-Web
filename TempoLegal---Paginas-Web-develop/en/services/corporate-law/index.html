Answers before you engage us.

The scope is defined for your company and the decision you need to make.

What does corporate and company law cover?

It covers the company’s structure and legal decisions: incorporation, corporate governance, shareholder agreements, meetings, minutes, powers of attorney, appointments, contracts and corporate changes. We can support a specific action or operations on a recurring basis. Before engagement, we define which actions you need and which matters require a specific service.

Should I incorporate a SAS or choose another structure?

A SAS is an alternative, but it is not assumed to suit every project. We review the activity, participants, management, investment and operational needs to assess the structure. If you are looking for a standard SAS incorporation, you can consult its dedicated page; foreign investment or a group structure requires its own review.

Can you help if the company is already incorporated?

Yes. We can review a specific decision, update documents, support a corporate change or define recurring advice. The starting point is your current situation: articles, appointments, shareholders, contracts and what you need to resolve. You do not need to create another company to organize the existing one.

What is the difference between an amendment and a change of shareholders?

Not all changes follow the same procedure. An amendment changes the articles; a share transfer, appointment or update may have different rules and registrations. We review the action, type of company, necessary decisions and documents before proposing a route.

Does monthly advice include all the firm’s services?

Not automatically. The recurring proposal identifies companies, matters, actions, time commitment and conditions. Structuring projects, court proceedings, administrative defence, accounting, DPO services or other procedures may be engaged with their own scope. We do not present a monthly fee as unlimited coverage.

Do you review and draft commercial contracts?

We can review, prepare or adjust contracts and support their negotiation when agreed. We specify the objective, parties, documents, versions and stages. Reviewing a contract does not mean taking on all negotiations or any later dispute; those aspects must be defined.

Can you help organize several companies or a holding company?

Yes. We first understand ownership, each company’s activity, relationships, management and the group’s objectives. A holding structure or reorganization must meet a real need and consider its corporate, contractual and tax effects. It does not in itself guarantee tax savings or absolute asset protection.

Do I need to be Ecuadorian or hold a visa to be a partner or shareholder?

No. You do not need to be Ecuadorian or hold a visa or residence in Ecuador to be a partner or shareholder in an Ecuadorian company. You may participate from abroad, meeting documentation and registration requirements. Residing, working or acting as legal representative are different situations and are reviewed separately.

Can you support a foreign company seeking to operate in Ecuador?

Yes. We review whether you need an Ecuadorian company, a branch of the foreign company or another appropriate structure. We connect that decision with the contracted representation, registered address and operational support. Soft Landing has a general page explaining that journey and linking to each specific service.

What information do you need for an initial conversation?

A description of the activity, the decision you want to make, the company’s status and whether there is a relevant date. You do not need to attach case files or credentials to the initial form. We will then indicate the appropriate documents and the channel for reviewing them.

How are fees and timeframes determined?

They depend on the action or service, company structure, available documents, participants, volume of work and procedures involving third parties. The proposal identifies deliverables, fees, expenses and starting conditions. We do not apply the timeframe or price of a standard SAS incorporation to every corporate project.

Does legal advice include accounting or legal representation?

These are separate services that can be coordinated. Legally analysing an obligation does not mean maintaining accounting records or accepting an appointment as representative. If you need those areas, we define the scope, responsibilities and conditions of each service.
