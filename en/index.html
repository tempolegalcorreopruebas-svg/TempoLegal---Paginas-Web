Initial guidance is free. If your matter needs additional analysis or services, we explain the scope and provide a proposal where appropriate.

Can you assist me from another country?+

Yes. We offer remote support for local and foreign companies as well as in-person consultations in Quito. We review each matter’s needs and documentation.

Can you coordinate legal and accounting work?+

We bring legal, accounting and technology expertise together according to your operational needs. We agree the scope with you before starting.
