This Policy explains who processes your personal data, for what purposes, on what legal grounds, with whom it is shared, how long it is retained and how to exercise your rights, under the Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP), its General Regulations and the resolutions of the Superintendence of Personal Data Protection (SPDP).

I. Data controller

LEGALTEMPO S.A.S., with tax identification number (RUC) 1793241972001, which provides services under the TempoLegal brand, is the controller of the personal data covered by this Policy. Address: Av. de los Shyris N32-218 y Eloy Alfaro, Edificio Parque Central, Oficina 508, Quito, Ecuador. Telephone: 097 888 7392 / 099 829 9370. Email: info@tempolegal.com.

Website and contact channels. LEGALTEMPO S.A.S. determines the purposes and means of processing on www.tempolegal.com and its subdomains, forms, WhatsApp, contact email, social media, newsletters and events, publication of testimonials and images, management of enquiries and proposals, conflict-of-interest checks and handling of requests related to these processing activities.

You may exercise your rights and submit enquiries or complaints to LEGALTEMPO S.A.S. at info@tempolegal.com or at the address indicated. The company handles these requests through its authorized personnel.

Contracted services. LEGALTEMPO S.A.S. provides services under the TempoLegal brand and is the controller of processing whose purposes and means it determines to manage the professional relationship and fulfil its obligations. When it processes data exclusively on behalf of a client and under its instructions, it will act as a processor, and the terms of the engagement and the policy of the client acting as controller will apply.

Privacy contact. Personal data enquiries and requests are handled at info@tempolegal.com.

This Policy does not apply to data we process on behalf of clients as processors, information we receive as a Data Protection Officer or as legal representatives of other organizations, or our personnel’s data. In those cases, the responsible organization’s policy or an internal personnel notice applies.

II. Principles and applicable legislation

We process your data lawfully, transparently and confidentially, for specified purposes and using only necessary information. We seek accuracy, limit retention and apply security measures proportionate to risk, under the LOPDP and its General Regulations.

You may request information about that assessment and object by writing to info@tempolegal.com. Objections to direct marketing are addressed immediately without requiring justification. Conditions and legal exceptions apply to other processing. Simply visiting the website does not authorize tracking advertising.

We do not use your data for purposes incompatible with those stated. If a new purpose is necessary, we will inform you beforehand and request your consent where applicable.

Automated decisions and profiling. We do not make decisions based solely or partly on automated assessments that produce legal effects or affect your rights and freedoms. Google and Meta may create interest profiles to show you TempoLegal advertising, and Clixtell may automatically exclude IP addresses associated with invalid clicks from our advertising, without legal effects for you. You may object through the channels in section XI.

Artificial intelligence. We may use artificial intelligence tools to assist drafting, review and analysis under professional supervision. Legal judgment remains with the TempoLegal professional. Where a tool may receive personal data or confidential information, we limit inputs to what is strictly necessary and use services and settings that prevent the information being used to train the provider’s models, alongside the appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards. We do not use these tools to make automated decisions about people. If a channel includes an automated assistant, we will inform the user. Such processing is subject to Resolution SPDP-SPD-2026-0009-R, as amended by Resolution SPDP-SPD-2026-0037-R.

VIII. Security

We apply technical, organizational and legal measures proportionate to risk: professional secrecy and personnel confidentiality commitments, need-to-know access, encrypted connections (HTTPS), anti-spam protection for forms and providers with recognized security standards. If a security breach occurs, we notify the authorities and, where applicable, affected data subjects under articles 43 and 46 of the LOPDP. If you detect a possible incident, contact info@tempolegal.com.

IX. Your rights

You may exercise, free of charge, your rights of access, rectification and updating, erasure, objection (particularly to legitimate-interest processing and newsletters), annulment, portability, restriction of processing, and not to be subject to decisions based solely or partly on automated assessments, including profiling, that produce legal effects or affect your fundamental rights and freedoms. You may also withdraw consent at any time, without retroactive effect.

Write to info@tempolegal.com with the subject “Data protection – exercise of rights”, or submit a written request at our office. No form is required.

State your name, the right you are exercising, what you request and where you wish to receive a response, and attach proof of identity or, if acting for another person, proof of representation (RGLOPDP, arts. 12 and 13). If you are already sufficiently identified through your chosen channel, we will not request further documents. This information will be used solely to manage the request.

We will respond within a maximum of 15 days. If your request is unclear, we will ask you once, within a period of 5 days, to clarify it; you will have a period of 10 days, counted from the day after that request, to do so.

Portability is addressed by providing your data in a structured, commonly used format (for example, .xlsx or .csv) or transmitting it to another controller where applicable.

Certain rights do not apply in the situations in article 18 of the LOPDP, for example where data is necessary to fulfil a legal obligation, including professional secrecy, or defend claims, or where fulfilling a request would harm third-party rights. In that case, we will explain the reasons.

Complaints. You may submit any complaint to info@tempolegal.com. If you disagree with our response, do not receive it within 15 days or believe your rights have been infringed, you may file a claim or complaint with the Superintendence of Personal Data Protection through its official channels (www.spdp.gob.ec).

X. Website tools and calculators

If you use a tool or calculator, we process the responses you enter to generate the requested result. Avoid including unnecessary names, identification details or sensitive information. If you request delivery of the result or ask us to contact you, we use contact details to handle that request; advertising requires a separate choice. Where personal data is collected, the tool will explain its processing and retention before requesting it.
