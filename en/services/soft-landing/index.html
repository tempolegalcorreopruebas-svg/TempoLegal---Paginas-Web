Conocer el soporte continuo ↓

The whole journey. A scope suited to you.

Define the structure: local companies with foreign individuals or companies as shareholders, foreign-company branches, articles, shareholder agreements, powers, authorizations, group structures and holdings where appropriate. A SAS is not assumed suitable for every project.

Establish and enable: incorporation or branch establishment, appointments, legal representation, tax address, RUC, signatures, invoicing and agreed banking or sector procedures. Establishing a branch and providing a tax address are different services. Third-party costs are identified.

Definir la estructura.

Operate and maintain continuity: accounting, tax, recurring company obligations, contracted legal support, commercial contracts, employment matters, administrative and document back office, intercompany coordination, responsible people and head-office reporting. Each stage has its own deliverables and terms.

  • Compañía local con personas o sociedades extranjeras como accionistas.

  • Establecimiento de sucursal de una compañía extranjera, cuando sea la ruta adecuada.

  • Estatutos, acuerdos de socios, facultades y mecanismos de autorización.

  • Estructuras de grupo y holdings cuando respondan al proyecto empresarial.

¿Compañía local o sucursal?

Son alternativas distintas en su estructura y relación con la empresa extranjera. Revisamos actividad, propiedad, control, documentos y necesidades operativas antes de recomendar una ruta. Una SAS es una posibilidad; no la asumimos como solución para todos los proyectos.

Establecer y habilitar.

Convertimos la estructura acordada en un plan de puesta en marcha, con seguimiento de cada frente.

  • Constitución o domiciliación de sucursal, documentación y nombramientos.

  • Representación legal y domicilio fiscal, según las necesidades de presencia local.

  • RUC, firmas y habilitación para facturar, conforme al encargo.

  • Carpeta y coordinación bancaria; permisos o gestiones sectoriales que se definan.

Domiciliación de una sucursal y domicilio fiscal resuelven necesidades diferentes. La propuesta identifica las gestiones aplicables y los costos de terceros.

Before you engage us

The scope is defined for your company and the decision you need to make.

Must I be Ecuadorian or have a visa to be a shareholder?

No. Foreigners can participate from abroad subject to identification, documentation and registration requirements. Shareholding does not automatically authorize residence or work. Immigration, employment, representation, signatures, powers and incorporation routes are reviewed for each case.

What does Soft Landing include?

Support to choose how to establish the business, manage setup and organize operating support. Depending on the project: structure, incorporation or branch establishment, representation, tax address, banking, legal, accounting and administration. The proposal defines stages, deliverables, responsible people and recurring services.

Do I need a new company or a foreign-company branch?

It depends on activity, ownership, parent-company commitments and operational needs. The two alternatives differ and require review before a recommendation. A SAS is not assumed suitable for every case.

Can you help if I do not already have a company abroad?

Yes. We can start with a founder’s or investor’s project, reviewing activity, participants and operating support. Personal residence procedures and personal bank accounts require a separate analysis and are not presumed included.

Does the service include opening a business bank account?

Documentation and bank coordination can be included in the agreed scope. The financial institution controls requirements, checks and approval. The proposal identifies our tasks and the company’s required actions; approval and a universal timeframe are not guaranteed.

How do tax address, representation and branch establishment differ?

The tax address concerns the applicable business address; representation concerns the powers and responsibilities of the person acting for the company. Establishing a foreign-company branch is a distinct process. Each service is scoped according to the project.

Can support continue after incorporation?

Yes. Recurring legal, accounting and administrative services specify tasks, responsible people and coordination with your team. Setup and later support have separate terms; incorporation does not automatically include all monthly services.

What does establishment cost and how long does it take?

It depends on the structure, entities, available documents, third-party procedures and ongoing support. Following review, the proposal identifies stages, professional fees and relevant external costs. An integral service does not automatically use a standard SAS price or timeframe.

Can you help a business already operating in Ecuador?

Yes. We can address a specific need or ongoing company organization, representation, contracts, accounting and defined administrative tasks. We first understand what works and the existing team; a new incorporation is not required.
