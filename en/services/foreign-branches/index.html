ANTES DE ELEGIR LA RUTA

Before opening a branch in Ecuador.

Documents, representation and operations: what you need to resolve before commissioning the process.

What is a branch of a foreign company in Ecuador?＋

A branch allows a company incorporated abroad to operate in Ecuador through an authorized local presence. It is not the same as incorporating a new Ecuadorian company with the parent as shareholder. We assess whether retaining the foreign company’s legal continuity suits the project’s contractual, corporate and operating model.

What is the difference between a branch and an Ecuadorian company with foreign capital?＋

With a branch, the foreign company operates through its Ecuadorian establishment. The alternative is an Ecuadorian legal entity whose shareholders may be foreign individuals or companies. Documentation, governance, parent-company relationships and obligations differ. We recommend a route after reviewing the business, control and project requirements, not merely the processing time.

¿Qué documentos debe preparar la matriz?＋

We typically review incorporation documents and current articles of association, proof of legal existence, the decision of the competent body to establish the branch, planned activities, allocated capital and the representative’s power of attorney. The exact checklist depends on the country, company and activity. We explain which documents to obtain, who must approve them and the formalities needed before issuance.

Do foreign documents require an apostille and translation?＋

We determine whether apostille or legalization applies based on the origin and nature of each document. If it is issued in another language, we assess the translation required for legal effect in Ecuador. Requirements vary by document. Preparing the parent company’s file correctly helps avoid reissuance and insufficient formalities.

¿Necesitamos un apoderado o representante en Ecuador?＋

Establishing a branch requires reviewing permanent representation in Ecuador and the powers required by corporate law. The power of attorney must cover both the procedure and operations; it is not merely a mailing contact. If a foreign representative is proposed, we also assess their eligibility for the role. Ongoing representation and its responsibilities are contracted separately.

Must I be Ecuadorian or have a visa to be a shareholder? What about representing the branch?＋

No. You do not need Ecuadorian citizenship, a visa or residence in Ecuador to be a shareholder of an Ecuadorian company. Foreigners may participate from abroad subject to applicable identification, documentation and registration requirements. This does not automatically grant permission to live or work in Ecuador. Acting as a legal representative or branch attorney-in-fact involves different requirements. In a branch, shareholders hold shares in the foreign parent; being a parent-company shareholder differs from acting as its representative in Ecuador.

¿Qué capital debe asignarse a la sucursal?＋

The parent company must allocate capital to its Ecuadorian activity in line with current requirements and sector-specific rules. This is separate from professional fees and procedural costs. We review the amount, evidence and required steps before drafting the parent company’s resolution; no single amount applies to activities subject to special rules.

How long does establishment take and how is it priced?＋

The schedule depends on documentation from the country of origin, required formalities, notarization, regulatory reviews, registrations and permits. The proposal specifies stages, deliverables, professional fees and third-party costs. We do not guarantee processing times or apply standard SAS incorporation timelines to foreign branches.

Does the service include tax registration and a bank account?＋

Registration and obtaining the tax ID (RUC) may be included in the establishment engagement. Preparation of the banking file and banking coordination depend on the project. Each bank checks its requirements and decides on account opening; establishing a branch does not guarantee an account. Powers of attorney, participants and sources of funds must align with the business.

Is establishing a branch the same as obtaining a tax address?＋

No. Establishing or registering a branch organizes the foreign company’s legal presence. A tax address is the address used for the relevant operational purposes. Contracting an address does not replace branch authorization or provide an operational office, warehouse or sector permits.

¿Pueden acompañarnos después de establecer la sucursal?＋

Yes. We can propose representation, tax address, accounting, employment obligations, contracts and task-based administrative support. The proposal specifies what we handle, what the parent company decides and what information its team provides. Recurring services are not automatically included in establishment and do not remove the company’s responsibilities.

Can all business activities be carried out through a branch?＋

The business activity and sector must be assessed before selecting a route. Some operations require special authorizations, capital levels or structures. Corporate registration does not replace any required licenses. If a branch is unsuitable, we compare incorporation of an Ecuadorian company or another option through a tailored proposal.
