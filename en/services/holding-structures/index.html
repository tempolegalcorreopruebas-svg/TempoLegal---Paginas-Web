Before you engage us

The scope is defined for your company and the decision you need to make.

What is a holding and what can it do?

A company holding interests in other companies may help organize group ownership, decision rules or relationships. We first review its objective. The holding label does not itself resolve management or replace each company’s obligations.

Do several companies necessarily need a holding?

No. Ownership, assets, activities, relationships and intended changes are reviewed first. Adjusted documents or agreements may be sufficient; other cases justify a different structure. The recommendation follows the operation, not a standard chart.

Can you organize a group already operating?

Yes. We review existing articles, ownership, management, contracts and accounting status to define a route. Implementation may require specific actions, agreements or transfers; all companies do not need to be recreated.

Does a holding guarantee lower taxes?

No. Effects depend on structure, actual activity, transactions and applicable requirements. Legal and tax-accounting analysis precedes recommendations. Additional entities also create obligations and maintenance costs.

Does a holding protect every asset from any debt?

No absolute protection is offered. Obligations, guarantees, liabilities and third-party rights remain relevant. Ownership arrangements require a legitimate purpose and coherent documentation; debts, litigation and contingencies must be expressly reviewed.

Must the holding be a SAS?

No single legal form is assumed. Participants, activity, governance, operation and restrictions determine the vehicle. Standard SAS incorporation is a different service; a group structure requires its own proposal.

What do you review between group companies?

Ownership, management rules, each entity’s functions, agreements, authorizations and intercompany documentation. Invoicing, accounting and evidence may be coordinated within scope; a comprehensive audit of every company is not assumed.

Do you include shareholder agreements and decision rules?

Relevant articles, agreements and approval mechanisms can be drafted or reviewed. We define each instrument and its coordination with management. Internal protocols do not remove legal responsibilities or replace required formalities.

Can you prepare new shareholder entry or group continuity?

Yes, by reviewing participation and decision documents. Family continuity may require specific company, tax and estate analysis. We do not offer a universal succession solution or presume every estate action included.

Do shareholders need Ecuadorian nationality or a visa?

No, subject to applicable documentation and registration. Residence, work and legal representation are different situations reviewed separately.

What are the deliverables and timeframe?

The proposal specifies analysis, alternatives, contracted documents and procedures, people and stages. Timing depends on information, company decisions, documents and third parties; there is no universal group-reorganization deadline.

What do you need to prepare a proposal?

Initially, the objective, number of entities, related countries and issue to organize. Articles, ownership tables, contracts and background are requested later through the indicated channel. Do not send private estate information, credentials or documents through the public form.
