DECIDE WITH INFORMATION

Answers before
engaging us.

The scope is defined for your company and the decision you need to make.

What does monthly legal advice for businesses include?＋

The proposal specifies the routine matters and tasks your company needs: consultations, document review or drafting, corporate, employment and contractual issues, meetings and follow-up. It also defines covered entities, time commitment, communication channels and responsible people. We do not offer identical, unlimited coverage for every operation.

Is this a block of hours or a defined-scope service?＋

The model depends on the business. Some proposals allocate monthly hours, while others define tasks and recurring coordination. Before engagement, we clarify what is included, how work is recorded, how excess time is handled and whether unused hours carry over. The arrangement is set out in your company’s proposal.

Can you act as our external legal department?＋

We can serve as your legal coordination point or complement an in-house lawyer or team. The proposal specifies who receives requests, how priorities are set and which matters are covered. Your company retains business decisions and authorizations; advice does not replace management.

What contracts can you review or draft?＋

Contracts and operational documents are covered according to the agreed subject matter, complexity and expected workload. We specify whether the service includes review, drafting, amendments or negotiation support. An exceptional transaction or different workload is assessed before including it in the monthly engagement.

Does the service include coming to our offices and attending meetings?＋

The service can include online or in-person meetings and work at your premises if relevant and agreed. Frequency, duration, travel and allocation of that time are specified in the proposal, together with the meeting schedule or scheduling arrangements.

What happens if a matter goes beyond the agreed scope?＋

We identify the request and assess with you whether it requires additional time, an expanded scope or a separate project. Approval and billing terms must be agreed before extra work begins. Government fees, notarial expenses and other third-party costs are separate from professional fees.

Does the monthly fee include court cases or administrative defense?＋

They are not automatically included. Court representation, defense before an authority, employment termination authorization or other proceedings may require a separate engagement. Routine advice can identify the need and coordinate next steps, but representation and its terms are confirmed separately.

Can you support several companies in the same group?＋

Yes, provided the proposal identifies the covered entities, their needs and coordination arrangements. An agreement with one company does not automatically cover all affiliates or shareholders’ personal matters. If an entity is added or the work changes, we review the scope first.

Does it include accounting, payroll or a data protection officer?＋

These services can be coordinated but require separate scopes. Legal advice may address corporate, employment or privacy matters without undertaking accounting records, payroll processing or a data protection officer appointment. If you need an integrated solution, we distinguish each service and its responsibilities.

How are requests and outstanding matters organized?＋

We agree on contacts, channels and a follow-up approach suited to your business. We identify each request’s objective, necessary information, priorities and next steps. Reports or follow-up meetings and their frequency are defined in the engagement; we do not imply that an uncontracted portal or system exists.

Can you review matters that were already pending before engagement?＋

Yes, following an initial review of their status, documents and needs. Extensive regularization or an existing dispute is not automatically absorbed into a new monthly service. We distinguish routine matters from those requiring a separate project or budget.

How does the service start and end?＋

We first understand your business and check for potential conflicts of interest. We then propose scope, fees, start conditions, term and coordination. The contract defines termination notice, outstanding information and handover of documents. If appointments or legal representation are included, replacing or terminating them requires the relevant procedures.
