ANTES DE ELEGIR LA RUTA

Invest and incorporate in Ecuador with clear information.

Shareholders, documents and control: what to settle before incorporation.

What does incorporating a company with foreign capital mean?＋

It means incorporating an Ecuadorian legal entity with foreign individual or corporate shareholders. The project may involve one or more investors, a relationship with a parent company and specific control rules. We define the structure before filing and distinguish incorporation from subsequent operational setup.

Do I need to be Ecuadorian or hold a visa to be a partner or shareholder?＋

No. You do not need Ecuadorian nationality, a visa or residence in Ecuador to be a shareholder of an Ecuadorian company. Foreigners may participate from abroad subject to applicable identification, documentation and registration requirements. This does not automatically grant permission to reside or work in Ecuador. Legal representation is a separate role with its own requirements.

Can a foreign company be a shareholder of the Ecuadorian company?＋

Yes, subject to applicable requirements and restrictions. We review legal existence, corporate documents, representation, ownership chain and information identifying those who own or control the structure. Simply entering the parent company’s name on a form is insufficient. Overseas documentation is prepared for the selected incorporation process.

¿Es obligatorio tener un socio ecuatoriano?＋

Foreign ownership alone does not mean an Ecuadorian shareholder must be added. However, the company type, required number of shareholders and restrictions related to the activity or location must be examined. We do not promise the same shareholder structure for all sectors.

Should we choose a SAS, a public limited company or a limited liability company?＋

It depends on the activity, investors, control arrangements, rules for transferring ownership and expected growth. A SAS is one option, not an automatic answer. We review who invests, who manages and how the parent company wants to make decisions before recommending the legal form and preparing documents.

How does this differ from establishing a branch?＋

An Ecuadorian company has its own legal personality and its investors hold shares or ownership interests. A branch is an authorized presence of the foreign company in Ecuador. Comparing the two requires reviewing contracts, control, documents, liability and operational continuity. You can consult our branch-services page and discuss both options with our team.

What documents do I need if I invest personally from abroad?＋

We review current identification, contact and address details, business activity, shareholding, capital and management. Depending on the process, electronic signatures or powers of attorney may be required. Residence or work authorization in Ecuador is assessed separately from shareholder status. Sensitive documents are submitted through the designated channel after the initial consultation.

What additional documents are needed for a foreign corporate shareholder?＋

We generally review proof of legal existence, corporate documents, representatives or attorneys-in-fact and shareholder information through to the individuals who must be identified. We determine apostille, legalization and translation requirements. The Ecuadorian company may also have ongoing reporting obligations concerning its foreign corporate shareholders; this extends beyond initial filing.

Can you handle the process without us traveling to Ecuador?＋

Much of the work can be coordinated with overseas participants and advisers. The precise process depends on the shareholders, company type, documents, signatures and powers of attorney. We cannot guarantee that all structures involving foreign entities can be handled on the same automated platform or without in-person checks by third parties.

¿Qué incluye la propuesta y cómo definen honorarios y tiempos?＋

The engagement may cover reviewing the structure, articles of association and decisions, foreign documents, powers of attorney, appointments, incorporation, registrations and tax ID (RUC), as well as agreed initial permits. The proposal defines stages, responsibilities, third-party expenses and recurring services. Document volume and complexity determine fees and timelines; standard SAS pricing does not apply here.

¿Incluye cuenta bancaria, domicilio y contabilidad?＋

These activities can be coordinated according to operational needs, with each scope defined in the proposal. We prepare banking documentation and support the agreed application, but the bank decides whether to open an account. Tax address, representation, accounting and administrative support have their own terms and responsibilities and are not automatically included indefinitely with incorporation.

¿Podemos incorporar inversionistas o reorganizar la estructura después?＋

Yes, the project may evolve. We review shareholder entries and exits, capital increases, shareholder agreements and management changes in light of the company type and its documents. A share transfer is not automatically an amendment to the articles of association. Subsequent changes are handled under a separate engagement, with relevant registers, records and information kept up to date.
