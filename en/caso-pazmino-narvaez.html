Case study

The challenge

Corporación Médica Pazmiño & Narváez brings together a clinic, laboratory, pharmacies, aesthetic centers, plastic surgery and occupational examinations. The challenge was to organize information processing across its different healthcare units.

The scope covered clinical records, prescriptions, examinations, laboratory results, diagnostic images, patient data, and occupational and administrative information.

Case study

The support

Support included implementing and monitoring the data protection system, reviewing clinical record processes and delivery of results.

Access to sensitive information, incident management, lawful bases and internal confidentiality measures were also reviewed.

Case study

Published outcome

According to the published case, the outcome was a clearer structure for integrating data protection into the group’s medical and administrative operations, strengthening confidentiality, internal controls and responses to patients, audits, incidents or requests.
