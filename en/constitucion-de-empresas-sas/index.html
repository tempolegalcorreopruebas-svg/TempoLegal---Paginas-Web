Your company registered. Your documents organized.

A SAS is a simplified joint-stock company with one or several shareholders. Lawyers review ownership, capital, administration, name, purpose and actual activities before preparing the incorporation deed, articles and appointment.

We manage filing and follow-up to registration, the appointment, RUC and institutional access, and configure the SRI’s free electronic invoicing system.

How many shareholders will your SAS have?

What will the company actually do?

We define its corporate purpose and register its actual activities, explaining the obligations and permits involved. The structure must reflect the real business.

How will shareholders make decisions?

We review ownership, management and decision-making under the agreed structure. Shareholder agreements or complex structures are quoted separately when needed.

Is a SAS right for you?

A SAS may be a flexible option for one or several shareholders. It is not suitable for every regulated activity and does not automatically provide tax savings. We can also assess a limited company, corporation or holding structure.

Before you engage us

The scope is defined for your company and the decision you need to make.

What is included from USD 140 + VAT?

A SAS with one individual shareholder: prior advice, structure, lawyer-reviewed documents, registration and appointment, RUC, SRI invoicing configuration, digital file, share certificates and register, bank documents and obligation guidance. Missing electronic signatures are separate; five or more shareholders or corporate shareholders require a specific quotation.

What is payable beyond incorporation fees?

VAT and any missing valid electronic signatures, from USD 25 + VAT for 30 days in Ecuador or USD 45 + VAT abroad subject to validation. Capital belongs to the company. Permits, municipal fees, monthly accounting and other services are not assumed included; scope and amounts are confirmed.

How long does incorporation take?

Expected registration and RUC timing is 24–48 business hours after complete documents and signatures, subject to Superintendencia and SRI availability. Pending documents, signature checks, corporate shareholders and other situations may need a different route and schedule, explained before starting.

What information is required?

Initially: shareholder count, actual activity and two or three names. The procedure requires identification, a voting certificate where applicable or passport, email, phone, address, signatures, capital, administration and legal representative. Do not submit identity documents, certificates, signature files or passwords in the public form.

Can one shareholder create a SAS and what capital is needed?

Yes, one or several shareholders are possible. There is no mandatory legal minimum capital; structures from USD 1 are considered. We review what capital and payment method suit the project. Capital is separate from professional fees.

Must shareholders be Ecuadorian or have a visa?

No. Foreigners may participate from abroad subject to documentation and registration. Shareholding does not automatically authorize living or working in Ecuador. Legal representation or relocation requirements are reviewed separately.

Can I do it from another city or abroad?

The standard electronic process can be coordinated remotely without visiting our office. For foreign participants we review identification and signature compatibility. Foreign corporate shareholders or additional documentation require a specific route and budget; one automatic form does not fit every case.

If the official procedure is free, why pay for advice?

Standard SAS registration and the RUC have no official fee. TempoLegal charges for professional review, documents, procedure management and organized delivery. You may apply independently. Our fees and any additional certificates or work are disclosed before engagement.

Will the SAS be ready to invoice and open a bank account?

RUC and SRI free invoicing configuration are included. The required signature and authorizations must remain valid. We supply bank-application documents, but the bank sets requirements and decides approval. Municipal and sector permits are separate from incorporation.

What happens if the Superintendencia raises an observation?

We follow up and correct observations related to the contracted incorporation, refiling without additional professional fees until registration. Shareholders must provide complete information and signatures. Structure changes or new work are scoped and priced before execution.

Is monthly accounting included and what happens afterward?

Monthly accounting is separate. You receive guidance on company, tax and municipal obligations and may choose later accounting, tax, payroll or other support. Incorporation does not itself determine a tax regime or guarantee lower taxes.

What if I need another company type or a complex structure?

We can advise on limited companies, corporations, foreign corporate participation, holdings and reorganizations. Activity, shareholders, management and objectives are reviewed. The SAS is not suitable for every regulated activity; alternative structures receive a separate proposal and do not automatically use the base fee.
