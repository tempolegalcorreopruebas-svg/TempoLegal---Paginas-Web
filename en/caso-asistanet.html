The context

Asistanet / Asista Centros Médicos is presented as a medical network case involving patient care, clinical records, appointments, internal platforms and medical providers. Its operations also include employee data and healthcare documentation.

Case study

Scope of the plan

The described plan addresses data processing, documentation and notices, alongside processes, providers, confidentiality, security and training for a private healthcare operation.

Case study

The objective

The objective of this plan is to provide documentary evidence and internal criteria for handling patient data and reducing risks of unauthorized access, lack of traceability or inappropriate processing of medical information.
