Review your sector’s cases before calculating

Appointing a DPO and large scale are different questions. In education, healthcare, financial activities, insurance and technology services, the applicable regulatory cases must be reviewed. In logistics and courier services the specific processing also matters. The score does not rule out a sector obligation or a direct large-scale case.

Not identifying a case does not prove an exemption. Review the linked sources and your operations with the legal team.

DPD is the Spanish name, Delegado de Protección de Datos; DPO stands for Data Protection Officer. They are two names for the same role.

1. The need for a DPO
Not identifying a case is not the same as being exempt.
Activities worth reviewing

Education and activities involving minors; finance and insurance; profile-based advertising; healthcare; pharmaceutical sector; private security and access control; professional sport and academies; trade associations; telecommunications; technology services; public service concessions.

The obligation depends on the specific case and, in some cases, on the legal form. The sector exception for independent health professionals does not remove other possible grounds. See article 10 of resolution 0028-R.

2. A specific processing operation

Calculate each use of data separately (for example, payroll management); do not indiscriminately add up clients, staff and providers from different processing operations.

What to review before calculating

The direct cases include certain health or sensitive processing, automated decisions with significant effects, systematic monitoring of public spaces, biometrics or geolocation, structural credit information, services aimed at minors, systematic transfers and courier services.

The isolated presence of a sensitive datum, a cloud provider or information on family dependants is not enough to characterize all those cases. Review the context and the rule with your adviser.
