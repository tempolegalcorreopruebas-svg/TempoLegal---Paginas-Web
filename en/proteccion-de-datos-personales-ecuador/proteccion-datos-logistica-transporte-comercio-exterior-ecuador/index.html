02 / EXTERNAL DPO

Independent advice and oversight.

When an appointment is required, an external DPO can advise on and oversee the data processing in your operations, coordinating with management, operations and IT.

In Ecuador the role is called DPD (Delegado de Protección de Datos). DPO stands for Data Protection Officer, its English name.

The DPO informs, advises and oversees; the organization decides and executes. The proposal defines the appointment, follow-up, products and separation of duties to prevent conflicts.

Explore the advantages of an external DPO
How do we determine whether we need a DPO?

Not every transport or logistics company is obliged just because it belongs to the sector. We assess your activity, processing, monitoring and the other applicable cases. The external DPO is one way of covering the role when required, independent from execution.

QUESTIONS TO DECIDE CLEARLY

What management and operations need to know.

Is this service for carriers, logistics operators or customs agents?

It can be adapted to freight transport, logistics, warehousing and related customs operations. We review the specific model: a carrier does not process data in the same way as an agent or an operator with several companies. The proposal defines the entities and activities covered; it is not comprehensive customs law advice.

Is every logistics company required to appoint a DPO?

It is not concluded from the sector’s name. The legal cases, processing and conditions of the operation must be reviewed. Contracting with the public sector does not in itself determine the concession or public-private partnership case either. See the SPDP’s non-binding technical opinion (Spanish)

Who is responsible for the data we receive from the client?

The role is determined for each processing operation. You may act on a client’s instructions for one operation and decide other uses of your own employment or commercial data. Contracts, instructions, recipients, subcontractors and purposes are reviewed; calling yourself a provider is not enough to settle all responsibilities.

What should we review in GPS and fleet monitoring?

If the location makes it possible to identify or track a person, the purpose, necessity, proportionality, information to staff, access and retention must be reviewed. Not every vehicle datum is automatically personal data; its relationship with drivers and other data subjects, the scope of monitoring and the provider are analysed. Geolocation of people can trigger a direct large-scale case; the processing and the corresponding impact assessment must be assessed.

Can we photograph an ID card or a delivery to keep proof?

You need to determine what evidence is necessary and proportionate. A full copy should not be collected out of habit if another record is enough. We review the data captured, the purpose, who has access, how it is delivered to the client and how long it is kept.

Does having BASC or security protocols cover the LOPDP?

There may be reusable controls and evidence, but it does not replace the data protection analysis. We review what already exists to avoid duplication. This service does not include BASC certification or audit unless a different scope is expressly contracted.

What happens if we subcontract transport, warehouses or technology services?

The real roles and flows, instructions, measures, assistance with rights requests and incidents, and conditions for return or deletion are identified. Each third party requires an assessment according to its access to and use of the data, not the same addendum signed indiscriminately.

Can one policy cover all the companies in the group?

There can be a common base, but each entity needs its own identification, processing, responsibilities and evidence. The proposal specifies what is shared and what is adapted; a single logo or a coordinated operation does not remove the legal separation.

How long does implementation take and what must we provide?

The schedule depends on entities, flows, systems, availability of information and the assessments needed. We coordinate with management, operations, human resources and IT, and review documentation and providers. Another company’s timelines do not automatically apply to your project.

Does it include changing our TMS, GPS or warehouse system?

Not by default. We review their use and the processing conditions. The supporting technical assessment is scoped in the proposal; development, configuration, penetration testing or certified audit are not included automatically.

Does the DPO replace the head of operations or the BASC officer?

No. The DPO advises and oversees independently. Operations, security, human resources and IT carry out their duties. Requirements and conflicts must be checked before combining roles, even if a person knows the business very well.

What do the support period and CumpliDatos include?

For implementation we offer 30 days from document delivery for questions and adjustments within the scope, at no extra cost. The DPO is a continuity service contracted separately; while you keep that service with TempoLegal, CumpliDatos is included at no extra cost to consult the RoPA (RAT), documents, training and the DPO’s reports, and to log data subject requests.
