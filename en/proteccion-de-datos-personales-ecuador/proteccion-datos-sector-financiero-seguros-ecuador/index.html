02 / EXTERNAL DPO

Independent advice and oversight.

Data protection advice and oversight through an external DPO, coordinated with management and the compliance, risk and IT functions.

In Ecuador the role is called DPD (Delegado de Protección de Datos). DPO stands for Data Protection Officer, its English name.

The DPO informs, advises and oversees; the organization decides and executes. The proposal defines the appointment, follow-up, products and separation of duties to prevent conflicts.

Explore the advantages of an external DPO
How do we determine whether we need a DPO?

The rules provide for sector-specific cases for legal entities with financial activity and access to or processing of data, and for insurance actors, including producing agents and brokers. The legal form, the activity and the specific case are reviewed; having few clients does not rule out a sector obligation. The external arrangement is not the only way to meet it. DPO Regulation, art. 10.4 and 10.5 · PDF (Spanish) ↗

QUESTIONS TO DECIDE CLEARLY

Answers for management and compliance.

Does this page include banks and insurance companies?

This service is aimed at savings and credit cooperatives, brokerage firms and insurance advisers or intermediaries. Each activity requires its own scope. Experience with banks or insurers is not attributed to the firm because it has worked with a credit union or a broker. Other activities require a specific scope assessment.

Does a savings and credit cooperative need a DPO?

There is a sector-specific case for financial activity, in addition to other criteria related to the processing. Entity, activity and processing are reviewed under the rules; you should not wait to have many members or a request from the authority to assess it. The obligation to appoint does not require the service to be external. SPDP technical opinion on credit unions (Spanish) ↗

Does the DPO obligation reach small insurance brokers and advisers?

The regulation includes insurance operations and actors such as producing agents and brokers. The entity and activity covered by the case must be verified, without ruling out a sector obligation just because of the number of clients. The proposal defines the appointment and duties according to your case; the external arrangement is not the only option.

Can the compliance officer also take on the DPO role?

The DPO regulation excludes the compliance officer and the information security officer from that role. Requirements, incompatibilities and duties must be reviewed before appointing. Coordination between areas is still necessary, with separate responsibilities. DPO Regulation, arts. 15 and 16 · PDF (Spanish) ↗

Does anti-money-laundering compliance replace data protection?

No. Existing controls, evidence and processes can be reused, but their purposes and obligations are not identical. The LOPDP project does not replace prudential compliance, sector reporting or the internal audit and information security functions either.

Can we ask for health data for a quote or a claim?

The purpose, necessity, legal basis and conditions applicable to health data must be analysed. It is not presumed that every medical file or history is necessary. Forms, information to the data subject, authorizations where appropriate, recipients, access and retention are reviewed. The same legal basis is not assigned to all insurance management.

Are the insurer, the broker and the platform all processors?

Not automatically. The roles depend on the decisions and the real use of the data in each activity. An insurer or an intermediary may act as controller for certain processing, and a provider may process on instructions in others. Contracts and operations must match those roles.

Must we delete a file when the client withdraws their consent?

It is not resolved with a single rule. The purposes, legal bases, retention obligations and applicable limitations must be identified, and the request answered with reasons. Nor is it appropriate to keep everything indefinitely. We define criteria by type of file and process.

Does scoring or profiling require an impact assessment?

When the legal cases apply, carrying out the impact assessment is mandatory; its scope must be identified in the project. The processing and the applicable cases are analysed. The large-scale rule covers structural processing in credit, financial or risk systems and certain automated profiling with significant effects. A numerical calculation does not rule out a direct case; not every commercial decision or isolated banking datum amounts to the same processing. Large-scale rule, art. 14 · PDF (Spanish) ↗

Can you make use of our current documents, controls and providers?

Yes. We review what exists and distinguish what to keep, correct or complete. The supporting technical review is scoped through agreed interviews, documents and evidence; it is not a security certification and does not automatically include penetration testing, configuration changes or replacing your systems.

What must our organization provide and how long does it take?

A management liaison and coordination with operations, compliance, human resources and IT, depending on the project. Processes, products, channels, providers and existing documents are reviewed. The schedule and the necessary assessments are confirmed in the proposal; another entity’s timeline does not carry over to your organization.

What do the 30 days of support and CumpliDatos include?

For implementation, 30 days from document delivery for questions and adjustments within the scope, at no extra cost. The DPO is contracted as a separate continuity service. While you keep the DPO service with TempoLegal, CumpliDatos is included at no extra cost to consult the RoPA (RAT), documents, training and the DPO’s reports, and to log data subject requests; it does not replace your business systems or guarantee compliance on its own.
