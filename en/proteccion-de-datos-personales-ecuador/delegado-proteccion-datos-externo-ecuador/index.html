SCOPE

What your external DPO does

The DPO informs, advises and oversees independently. Your organization remains responsible for making decisions and carrying out the appropriate measures.

01

Advice for your decisions

Guidance on data protection obligations and observations on the processing operations and changes your organization plans to make.

02

Oversight with follow-up

Review of progress on the agreed measures and communication of observations and priorities, at the frequency defined in the proposal.

03

Rights and relationship with the authority

Advice on handling requests from individuals and cooperation as the point of contact with the Superintendency of Personal Data Protection.

04

Risks and incidents

Advice and oversight on risks, impact assessments and incident response. Carrying out measures and notification obligations falls to those legally assigned to them.

RISKS WORTH ADDRESSING

What sanctions the LOPDP provides for

Non-compliance can lead to corrective measures and sanctions. Knowing the obligations and documenting how they are met allows you to manage the risk with information.

Private entities and public companies

Minor infringements

0,1 % a 0,7 %

Serious infringements

0,7 % a 1 %

Of the turnover for the financial year immediately preceding the one in which the fine is imposed. It is not calculated on profit. The SPDP determines the sanction through the corresponding procedure and applies proportionality criteria.

For public servants or officials, the law provides for 1 to 10 unified basic salaries for minor infringements and 10 to 20 for serious ones. Public companies follow the percentage ranges shown above.

Failing to appoint a DPO when required is classified as a serious infringement. We review whether your organization must appoint one and which arrangement and scope are appropriate. Hiring the service does not remove your responsibilities or guarantee the absence of sanctions.

WE ANSWER YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How are fines for breaching the LOPDP calculated?

For private entities and public companies, minor infringements are sanctioned with between 0.1% and 0.7%, and serious ones with between 0.7% and 1%, of the turnover for the financial year preceding the one in which the fine is imposed. The base includes sales of products and provision of services, net of VAT and other taxes directly related to the economic operation; it is not profit. The SPDP’s opinion states that it is not limited to income from Ecuador or to data processing operations. The sanction depends on the procedure and on proportionality criteria. For public officials: 1 to 10 unified basic salaries (SBU) for minor infringements and 10 to 20 SBU for serious ones.

Official SPDP source (Spanish)
Does not having a DPO mean an automatic fine?

No. First it must be determined whether your organization is required to appoint one. Failing to appoint one when required is classified as a serious infringement in article 68, item 12, of the LOPDP. A sanction requires the applicable procedure and the authority’s assessment. Not every organization is obliged just because it processes data. We review your activity and processing; appointing a DPO does not replace the measures your organization must carry out either.

See the LOPDP · PDF (Spanish)
How does an internal DPO differ from an external one?

The arrangement changes the type of relationship, but it does not remove the duties or the requirement of independence. We review requirements, time commitment and possible conflicts before proposing a person for the role.

Can I hire the service if I already have a data protection programme in place?

Yes. We review the starting point and define the scope of oversight. Hiring a DPO does not in itself require redoing the whole implementation; corrections or additional work are identified and quoted separately.

What factors determine the quotation?

We consider the entities and processing covered, the type and volume of data, the risks, the status of your measures and the time commitment required. The proposal sets out fees, frequency and deliverables before you hire us.

Do you serve organizations outside Quito?

We serve organizations throughout Ecuador from Quito. Coordination can be remote; visits and any on-site work are specified in the proposal where appropriate.

What is CumpliDatos and is it included in the service?

CumpliDatos is TempoLegal’s own platform for organizing tasks, tracking progress and gathering evidence of the work. It is included at no additional cost while you retain TempoLegal’s DPO service. It supports the DPO’s professional judgment; it does not replace implementation or guarantee compliance.

Is my organization required to appoint a DPO?

The obligation depends on the activity and the processing, including the cases set out in the LOPDP and the SPDP’s regulations. The criteria include permanent and systematic monitoring due to volume, nature, scope or purposes, and special categories of data on a large scale. Sector-specific cases must also be reviewed. Being a small organization does not exempt it on its own. The preliminary assessment gathers information to guide the analysis; it does not replace the legal determination of your case.

In healthcare, for example, there are cases linked to the obligation to keep medical records, with an exception for professionals in private practice; the other applicable cases are also reviewed. DPO Regulation, art. 10.7 (Spanish)

What does “from USD 95 plus VAT per month” mean?

It is a starting fee, not a universal price. It applies to small organizations with low-complexity processing and a scope compatible with that time commitment. The proposal identifies the entities and processing covered, the professional, frequency, deliverables, channels and fees. CumpliDatos is included while you keep the DPO service. The proposal distinguishes the included services from implementation, audits and additional technical work that must be contracted separately.

Is there a cost for the preliminary assessment?

No. It is personalized initial guidance based on the information you provide. It does not include an audit, certification or implementation, and it does not oblige you to hire us.

Can I answer the questions by WhatsApp?

Yes. After the first contact you can answer the ten questions in writing or by voice notes. Do not send databases, IDs, medical records or confidential documents.

Does the service include implementing the entire data protection system?

Hiring a DPO does not automatically include implementing the entire system. The proposal identifies the reviews, reports and audits included, the additional work and who carries it out, preserving the DPO’s independence.

Does an external DPO guarantee there will be no sanctions?

No. The service provides advice and oversight. Compliance also depends on your organization’s decisions, measures and actions.
