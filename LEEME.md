# TempoLegal website

Extract the ZIP and open `TempoLegal-Final/index.html`, or serve that directory with a static web server. No build step is required. Keep the directory structure and assets together.

The original presentation and team photographs are restored, with the approved October 2026 text incorporated into existing sections. See `UPDATE-NOTES.md` for the current change scope.

Spanish is at the root. English, Portuguese and French pages are in `en/`, `pt/` and `fr/`. Existing team, case, article and privacy pages remain part of the project. Terms and Legal Notice V1.2 are at `terminos-y-condiciones.html` in the supplied approved Spanish version.

`content.js` contains localized website copy. `config.js` defines contact channels. Legal source data and original Word documents are included. Existing generation utilities are retained for reference; do not run an older generator over this delivery without adapting it to the current content and additional case routes.
